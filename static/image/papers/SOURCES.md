# Publication media

Downloaded on 2026-09-28 from the papers or their authors' project pages. Figures
are stored locally as WebP, at up to 1000 pixels wide, with the complete figure
preserved. Transparent sources must be composited onto opaque white before
resizing or converting to RGB; dropping alpha directly can turn the background
black. DA-Nav was re-exported this way from its original RGBA PNG.
